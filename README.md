# formexposed – check your forms

The contact form protection of all your MainWP child sites, from formexposed.com, in your MainWP dashboard.

- Every child site with its formexposed rating (Exposed / Partially protected / Protected / No contact form found), risk score, last check, number of problems and bots blocked in the last 30 days
- A summary on top and a link to the full report of each site
- Marks child sites that are not in formexposed yet, and lists checked sites that are not in this dashboard
- The API key is checked before it is saved; errors are explained in plain words
- Without MainWP it works as a plain overview under Settings > formexposed portfolio

For agencies with a formexposed Business plan. Requires WordPress 6.2 and PHP 7.4. Details in [readme.txt](readme.txt).
